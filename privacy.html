Privacy Policy — Patient FY Auto-Update

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

Patient FY Auto-Update is a private Google Apps Script project run by one family for the home care of one family member. It is not a commercial service and has no other users. Home page.

What data we access

With the account holder's explicit consent, the app requests read-only access to the following Google Health API data: heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, oxygen saturation, weight, body fat and related health measurements; sleep; activity and steps; irregular-rhythm notifications; and electrocardiogram records. The app requests no write access and cannot change any data in Google Health.

How we use it

The data is used only to support this one person's care: it is copied once a day into a private Google Sheet in the family's Google Drive, where her caregivers and her doctors review trends and alerts. Caregivers may use AI assistant tools to summarise these trends for her care team.

The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone outside her caregivers and care team.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Data is stored only in the family's private Google Drive and Google Apps Script project, protected by Google account security. It is kept for as long as it is needed for her care and deleted on request.

Revoking access

The account holder can revoke this app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation no further data is read.
