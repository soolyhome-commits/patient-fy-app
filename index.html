Patient FY Auto-Update

A private, single-family home-care tool. Not a commercial product and not available to the public.

What it does

Patient FY Auto-Update is a Google Apps Script project run by one family to support the home care of one family member. With her consent, it reads her health measurements from the Google Health API (read-only) once a day and copies them into a private Google Sheet in the family's own Google Drive. Her caregivers and her doctors use that sheet to follow trends in heart rate, oxygen saturation, sleep and weight between appointments.

Who can use it

Only the one person whose data is monitored signs in to this app. It has no other users and no sign-up.
